The Jiangmen Underground Neutrino Observatory

JUNO is a 20-kiloton liquid scintillator detector under Dashi Hill near Kaiping, Jiangmen, Guangdong, China. About 650 m of rock (≈1800 m water equivalent) shields it from cosmic rays. It sits 52.5 km from both the Yangjiang and Taishan nuclear power plants, a baseline chosen to resolve the fine interference pattern of reactor antineutrino oscillations.

Target
20 kt linear alkyl benzene + 2.5 g/L PPO + 3 mg/L bis-MSB, in a 35.4 m acrylic sphere (263 panels, 12 cm thick)
Light detection
17,596 20" PMTs (4,939 dynode, 12,657 MCP) and 25,587 3" PMTs, ~78% coverage, ~1785 p.e./MeV at the centre
Veto
35 kt water Cherenkov pool (Ø 43.5 m × 44 m) with 2,752 20" + 600 8" PMTs, and a three-layer plastic-scintillator Top Tracker
Energy resolution
3.4% for the two 0.511 MeV γ at the centre; non-linearity known to better than 1%
Timeline
Construction from 2015, liquid scintillator filled by 22 Aug 2025, physics data taking since 26 Aug 2025
First result
sin²θ₁₂ = 0.3092 ± 0.0087 and Δm²₂₁ = (7.50 ± 0.12) × 10⁻⁵ eV² from 59.1 days, 1.6× better than all previous measurements combined

Its main goal is the neutrino mass ordering. JUNO also studies atmospheric, solar, geo- and supernova neutrinos, and searches for proton decay and other new physics.

Sources: JUNO Collaboration, arXiv:2511.14593 and arXiv:2511.14590 (Nov 2025). Official site: juno.ihep.cas.cn.

What you are looking at

Every event here is simulated. Two sources are available: a built-in generator running in your browser, or GENIE .gst.root files you open yourself, read locally with JSROOT.

  • Particles travel in straight lines (JUNO has no magnetic field), lose energy, shower, scatter, decay or are captured. Neutrons wander and are captured on hydrogen (2.223 MeV γ) after ~200 µs.
  • Light is emitted as isotropic scintillation plus a small Cherenkov cone, attenuated over 20.6 m in the scintillator, and turned into photoelectrons on each PMT with its timing spread.
  • Readout windows of 1008 ns follow JUNO's trigger (350 PMTs in 304 ns). Muon decays and neutron captures land in later windows, shown with compressed time.
  • Reconstruction is a simple charge-centroid vertex, energy from total p.e., and an entry/exit fit for muons. It is not JUNO's own reconstruction.

Approximations: PMT positions are a uniform layout with the real counts, not the engineering map; the built-in generator uses free-nucleon kinematics and approximate process shares; there is no refraction, reflection or LS non-linearity. Use GENIE files for anything quantitative.

Not an official product

This display is an independent project. It is not produced, reviewed or endorsed by the JUNO Collaboration, IHEP or any partner institution, and it shows no real JUNO data. Detector numbers come from the collaboration's public papers.
